PRODUCTION FILTERS
==================

- Price at least $20
- Average volume at least 1,000,000 shares
- Price at or above MA20
- MA20 at or above MA50
- RSI between 40 and 80
- Completed-session VolumeRatio at least 0.8
- MACD not materially below its signal line
- 63-session RelativeStrength at least -5

RELATIVE STRENGTH
=================

Available fields:

- RS21
- RS63
- RS126
- RS252
- RSComposite

Compatibility rule:
RelativeStrength remains equal to RS63.

RSComposite formula:

RSComposite = 0.15 * RS21
            + 0.50 * RS63
            + 0.25 * RS126
            + 0.10 * RS252

MARKET REGIME ENGINE
====================

- BULL = RegimeScore 15
- NEUTRAL = RegimeScore 7
- BEAR = RegimeScore 0

MarketScore remains an alias of RegimeScore.

OBSERVATIONAL FACTOR POLICY
===========================

The following remain observational:

- RSComposite
- Breakout55
- DistanceToHigh55

They must not affect production filters, Score Engine, TradePlan or candidate ranking without a separately validated production change.
